FROM LIST TO FLASHCARDS

A minute of setup.

  1. 1

    Bring your questions and answers

    Paste text or choose a UTF-8 CSV or TXT file. Separate each question and answer with a comma. Every record becomes a card, including duplicates.

    1,하나
2,둘
"Hello, friend",안녕

    Do not add a header. Use straight double quotes around fields containing commas or line breaks. Write a quotation mark inside a quoted field as "". Blank lines are ignored.

  2. 2

    Check your study set

    Give it a name and flip through the preview. Turn on reverse cards to practice the answer as a question, too.

  3. 3

    Take it to Goodnotes

    Choose Create study set, then Download study set. If your browser supports sharing this file, you can choose Share file. Find downloads in your browser or in Files → Downloads on iPhone and iPad. In Goodnotes, choose New → Import and select the .goodnotes file.

    Native exports use an unofficial, template-based format. This website’s exports have not yet been import-tested in Goodnotes on Apple hardware.

Everything is processed in this browser. Your card content is never sent to a server.
